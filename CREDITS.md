source code, and no shadcn components.

## Source note

B is a 1672×941 JPEG with a baked white background and no alpha channel. The
transparent master is reconstructed from the white matte. It is not recovered
original alpha. The cell geometry is measured from the source rather than
snapped to an ideal grid; B is close to, but not exactly, a perfect lattice.
